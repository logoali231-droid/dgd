## 1. CORE LOOP (Refined)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     12-HOUR SHIFT TIMER                          │
│  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │  [ORDER QUEUE]  →  [ACCEPT/DECLINE]  →  [NAVIGATE]        │  │
│  │        ↓                                      ↓           │  │
│  │  [PICKUP ZONE]  →  [TRANSIT/DODGE]  →  [DROPOFF ZONE]     │  │
│  │        ↓                                      ↓           │  │
│  │  [RATING UPDATE]  ←  [CUSTOMER INTERACTION]  ←  [TIP CALC]│  │
│  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
│                              ↓                                   │
│              [SHIFT END] or [DEACTIVATION]                       │
│                              ↓                                   │
│         [LOGBOOK ENTRY]  →  [META SHOP]  →  [NEW RUN]           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The Shift Structure:**
- **6 AM - 10 AM**: Morning Rush (Coffee orders, breakfast, high volume)
- **10 AM - 2 PM**: Lunch Surge (Food delivery, office buildings, elevator waits)
- **2 PM - 5 PM**: Dead Zone (Fewer orders, but "surge pricing" tempts you to keep going)
- **5 PM - 9 PM**: Dinner Chaos (Peak orders, angry customers, traffic hell)
- **9 PM - 12 AM**: Late Night (Drunk orders, sketchy neighborhoods, 2x tips)

Each shift phase has **different risk/reward ratios** and **unique customer types**.

## 5. GAMEPLAY MECHANICS (Detailed)

### Order Selection Phase
- Orders appear as **cards** with:
  - Restaurant name & distance
  - Customer distance & neighborhood type
  - Estimated tip (hidden until upgraded)
  - **Risk indicators** (⚠️ = known difficult customer)
  - Time limit

- **Acceptance Rate** drops if you decline too many orders
- **Surge pricing** events tempt you with 2x pay but 3x stress

### Navigation Phase
- **Top-down or isometric view**
- Swipe/tap to steer
- **Hazard types:**
  - 🚗 Cars (random door opens, sudden brakes)
  - 🚶 Pedestrians (jump out randomly)
  - 🐕 Dogs (chase you, steal food)
  - 🕳️ Potholes (damage bike, spill food)
  - 🌧️ Weather (rain = slippery, snow = slow)
  - 🚧 Construction (forced detours)

- **Mini-events:**
  - Red lights (wait = time lost, run = risk)
  - One-way streets (wrong way = cop attention)
  - Hills (energy drain)
  - Stairs (if delivering to apartment)

### Delivery Phase
- **Customer interaction options:**
  - Knock politely (safe)
  - Call phone (uses battery)
  - Text (slow, but low stress)
  - Leave at door (risk of theft complaint)

- **Customer archetypes:**
  - 😊 **The Gratful**: Auto 5-star, good tip
  - 😤 **The Impatient**: Timer is shorter, tips less
  - 🤓 **The Perfectionist**: Inspects food, complains easily
  - 🍺 **The Drunk**: High tip, but unpredictable
  - 👻 **The Ghost**: Doesn't answer door, you wait
  - 🤖 **The Bot**: Automated complaints, no tip

### Rating Calculation
```
Base Rating: 5.0
- Late delivery: -0.5 to -2.0
- Cold food: -0.5 to -1.5
- Damaged food: -1.0 to -3.0
- Rude interaction: -1.0
- Customer mood modifier: ±0.5
+ Apology bonus: +0.2 (if upgraded)
+ Perfect delivery: +0.1 (caps at 5.0)

Final Rating = Clamped 0.0 - 5.0
```

## 6. RANDOM EVENTS (The Algorithm's Wrath)

### "Surge" Events
- **"PEAK PAY!"**: 2x earnings, 2x order volume, 3x stress
- **"RAIN BONUS!"**: +$2 per delivery, but slippery roads
- **"ALGORITHM APPRECIATION DAY"**: Random +0.3 rating bonus (rare)

### "Penalty" Events
- **"AUDIT"**: Algorithm randomly checks your stats. Any stat below threshold = -0.5 rating
- **"CUSTOMER COMPLAINT WAVE"**: Multiple customers complain simultaneously
- **"APP UPDATE"**: Controls change mid-shift (adds confusion)
- **"GPS OUTAGE"**: Navigation disabled for 2 minutes

### "Opportunity" Events
- **"CASH TIP"**: Customer tips in cash (untaxed, instant)
- **"STREET FESTIVAL"**: Bonus orders, but crowded streets
- **"FELLOW COURIER"**: Another courier gives you a shortcut tip
- **"CHARGING STATION"**: Free phone charge if you stop

## 8. PROGRESSION & REPLAYABILITY

### Run Structure
- **Each run = 1 shift (12 hours / ~15-20 min real time)**
- Death = Deactivation = Logbook entry
- **Persistent currency**: "Saved Tips" (a fraction of earnings)
- **Meta-upgrades** make future runs easier but never trivial

### Difficulty Scaling
- **Shift 1-10**: Learning phase, forgiving algorithm
- **Shift 11-30**: Algorithm gets stricter, more hazards
- **Shift 31-50**: "Veteran Courier" status, rare order types
- **Shift 51+**: "Legendary Courier" mode, algorithm actively hunts you

### Endgame
- **"Perfect Shift"**: Complete 12 hours with 5.0 rating (extremely hard)
- **"Unionize"**: Secret ending where you organize couriers and fight the algorithm
- **"Deactivation #1000"**: Special logbook entry, unlocks "Ghost Courier" skin
